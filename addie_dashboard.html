House rules

Written once, meant to run everything — the constraints that don't change just because a feature gets added.

  1. Addie never touches money. She reads, charts, tests, and flags. Every real-world action is yours — deliberately.
  2. Paper before paper money. Ideas trade in simulation first. Real dollars only after an idea survives weeks of live paper-testing — and even then, small.
  3. Write it before you test it. A prediction logged after the fact is a story, not a test.
  4. Killed ideas stay visible. The rejected pile is part of the record, not deleted.
  5. The benchmark is a plain index fund. Any thesis that can't beat "just hold the market" after costs is a hobby, not an edge.
